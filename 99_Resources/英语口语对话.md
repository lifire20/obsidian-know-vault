## U 15 图表描述 P 135
**图表 1：消费品销售额（电影与电视，2011–2015 年）**
**Chart 1: Sales of Consumer Products (Movie Sales & TV, 2011–2015)**
 **A**:
This line graph illustrates the percentage changes in movie and TV product sales from July 2011 to July 2015.
Movie sales, the solid line, experienced dramatic wild fluctuations throughout the period. It peaked at nearly 60% in mid-2011, then dropped sharply, with multiple deep troughs close to zero or negative growth. After mid-2014, movie sales rebounded strongly and climbed to around 55% by 2015.
By contrast, the dotted line representing TV sales stayed far lower overall. TV sales grew gently to a peak of roughly 20% in late 2012, then fluctuated weakly and finally slipped back to negative growth in 2015.
Overall, movie products saw far bigger volatility and a stronger late-stage recovery than TV products.
这张折线图展示了从 2011 年 7 月到 2015 年 7 月期间，电影和电视产品销售额的百分比变化。 电影销售额（实线）在整个期间经历了剧烈的波动。它在 2011 年年中飙升至近 60%，随后急剧下降，期间多次跌至接近零或负增长的深谷。2014 年年中之后，电影销售额强劲反弹，到 2015 年攀升至 55%左右。 
相比之下，代表电视销售额的虚线整体保持在较低水平。电视销售额温和增长，在 2012 年末达到约 20%的峰值，随后出现微弱波动，最终在 2015 年回落到负增长。 总体而言，与电视产品相比，电影产品的波动性要大得多，且在后期出现了更强劲的复苏。

**Chart 2: Global Fast Food Cumulative Sales (1999–2014)**
 **图表 2：全球快餐累计销售额（1999–2014 年）**
 **B：**
The second line chart displays cumulative percentage growth of six categories of global fast food from 1999 to 2014.
Asian food dominated all categories by a huge margin. Its cumulative increase hit 482% in 2014, soaring steeply after 2008.
Chicken and Middle Eastern food ranked the second and third, with moderate and steady rises. Pizza, Burgers and Latin food grew slowly at similar mild rates.
